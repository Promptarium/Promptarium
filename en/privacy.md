# Promptarium — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30/10/2026

> Template provided for convenience; it is not legal advice. Have it reviewed before publication.

## Summary

Promptarium does not collect your personal data. The developer has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDK. Your prompts stay on your devices and in your own private iCloud.

## Who is responsible

Nicolas Mathis, Barcelona - SPAIN (the "developer"). Contact: promptarium@proton.me.

## Data stored by the app

Your prompts (title, text, notes, keywords), your lists of AIs and types, and your settings (language, favorites, usage counters) are stored on your device.

If you are signed in to iCloud, this data is synchronized between your devices through Apple's CloudKit, in your private database. It is tied to your Apple Account and is not accessible to the developer.

## Data the developer receives

None automatically. The developer does not receive your prompts, identifiers or usage data.

If you choose "Send feedback", the app opens your email app with a pre-filled message (app version, system version, language, access status). Nothing is sent until you send it yourself. The developer uses the email address and content only to answer you.

## Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple (App Store). The developer does not receive your payment details. The app checks your purchase status on your device through Apple's StoreKit.

## Free trial

To apply the 3-day trial across your devices and after reinstalling, the app stores the trial start date on your device and in iCloud key-value storage. It consists of a date and a random device identifier, with no personal information.

## Clipboard

The app writes to the clipboard when you copy a prompt. It reads the clipboard only when you tap "Paste" or "New from clipboard".

## Sharing and tracking

No data is sold or shared with third parties. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Retention and deletion

Your data remains until you delete it. Deleting a prompt removes it from all your devices through iCloud. Deleting the app removes the local copy; iCloud data can be managed in Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ iCloud ▸ Manage Storage. Feedback emails can be deleted on request.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may request access to, correction or deletion of the data the developer holds about you, which is limited to feedback emails. Contact promptarium@proton.me. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from them.
