Frequently asked questions

My prompts do not appear on another device. Check that you are signed in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on both devices, that iCloud is enabled for the app, and allow a few moments for synchronization.

The app is in read-only mode. The trial has ended and there is no active purchase. Open Settings ▸ See plans, or use Restore purchases.

How do I change the language? Settings ▸ Language. "Automatic" follows your device; if your language is unavailable, English is used.

How do I cancel my subscription? Settings ▸ [your name] ▸ Subscriptions on your device, or in the App Store account page.
